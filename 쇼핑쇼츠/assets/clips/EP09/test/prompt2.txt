Fixed ceiling security camera, the camera never moves, no zoom, no cut. Tiny figures far from the camera, subtle natural weight shifts, cloth fluttering, cushion fabric dents under their feet, no camera movement. Monochrome infrared night footage with noise. The tiny people strain to push the huge lint roller forward across the cushion: the roller rolls slowly about one body length, the fabric dents under it and under their boots, loose cat hairs stick to the drum and get pulled up off the ground. The man in the hard hat digs in step by step with real effort, shoulders heaving; the second person pushes with real effort, slips once and recovers; the small figure trips over a cat hair, falls flat, scrambles up and runs to catch up, pompom and loose thread swinging. Dust specks puff up where they step. The little lamb trots after them, bouncing. The sleeping cat breathes slowly, its ear twitches once, nothing else moves. Natural continuous human motion with weight and momentum, not stop-motion, not puppets, not toys being moved.
