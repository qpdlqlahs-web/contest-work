Fixed ceiling security camera, the camera never moves, no zoom, no cut. Monochrome infrared night footage with noise. The three tiny people strain to push the huge lint roller forward across the cushion: the roller rolls slowly about one body length, the fabric dents under it and under their boots, loose cat hairs stick to the drum and get pulled up off the ground. The man in the hard hat digs in step by step with real effort, shoulders heaving; the woman pushes with her back, slips once and recovers; the young man trips over a cat hair, falls flat, scrambles up and runs to catch up, his pompom and loose thread swinging. Dust specks puff up where they step. The little lamb trots after them, bouncing. The sleeping cat breathes slowly, its ear twitches once, nothing else moves. Natural continuous human motion with weight and momentum, not stop-motion, not puppets, not toys being moved.
